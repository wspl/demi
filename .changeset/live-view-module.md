The conversation browser serves the live view: `browser.live` streams the tabs a viewer watches as H.264 and takes the viewer's input, beside the agent's commands on the same tabs. A view opens tabs, closes them, navigates, answers dialogs, picks a tab's Web or Mobile mode, chooses in native controls and sends files through the stream, and its keys, buttons and composition are released when it ends.
