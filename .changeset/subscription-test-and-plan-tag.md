A subscription is tested from its active account: an icon button beside Remove, whose answer is a toast; an inactive account and a provider whose requests run on a conversation's Host (`runsOnHost`) have it disabled and say why. Adding an account is a button in the subscription's header, beside its switch, instead of a Sign in row. API-key providers keep their Test connection row. An account the vendor names no plan for shows no empty tag.
