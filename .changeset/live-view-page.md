The work panel shows the conversation browser's own tabs live: their pictures arrive as H.264 over the conversation's `browser` stream, and the user clicks, types, scrolls, pastes and answers dialogs in them while the agent works on the same tabs. A tab's viewport menu offers Web and Mobile and shows what the agent set, the page's own select, date, colour, suggestion and file controls open the viewer's pickers, chosen files travel through the stream, and a user operation reports conversation activity. Both kinds of browser tab share one address bar.
